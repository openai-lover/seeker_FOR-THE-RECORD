## Technical and eligibility gates

The entry needs an Android APK, Solana Mobile Stack/Mobile Wallet Adapter integration and meaningful Solana network use. A generic web wrapper is weak evidence of mobile development. The project must have started no earlier than three months before launch, with substantial new mobile work during the event. The creator must verify the actual start date and ownership.

Participants must meet age, residence and sanctions conditions. Only teams without VC or angel funding qualify for USDC prizes. Each contestant may submit once. The roster locks on final submission. Finalists must complete identity checks. Prize conditions can require an actual dApp Store listing within 30 calendar days of the announcement.

This is a preparation checklist, not an eligibility determination. Read [Terms §§3–6 and 9–10](https://solanamobile.radiant.nexus/legal/clock-in-terms.pdf) and provide truthful owner/team information.

## What the judges score

Each category carries 25%: product fit and repeat use, mobile usability, originality, and presentation/demo. The entry should show a complete daily loop, readable phone UI, a credible Solana-specific purpose and clear evidence of what works. This project has no measured retention or market validation yet, so describe repeat use as a hypothesis.

Source: [official portal, Information → Judges](https://solanamobile.radiant.nexus/).

## Submission route

Open [CLOCK IN](https://solanamobile.radiant.nexus/) → **HACKATHON HUB** → sign in to Align → complete the builder profile → Clock In Registration. The public FAQ says drafts can change before the deadline, but final submission agreement locks editing. Review all links and the fixed roster before that final action.
